# THE SPACE BETWEEN US
## Episode 03 — THE THINGS WE KEPT
**Season 1 | Episode 3 | Screenplay V1**

**Genre:** Sapphic Romantic Comedy / Drama
**Language:** English
**Estimated runtime:** 13 minutes, 15 seconds of scenes; ~14:30 with the silences (studio rule §13)

> **Status:** ✅ APROVADO (2026-11-22, N42). Base: escaleta V1 aprovada (N41), conceito B "Contact Sheet" (N40).
>
> **Regra de interpretação (§13, V70/V71), vale para toda fala entre Claire e Olivia:** ~2 palavras por segundo; pausa típica de 1,5–2 s entre as falas. As marcações *(a beat)* e *(a long beat)* no roteiro são silêncios de verdade, não sugestões. Só Maya e June são rápidas, e só quando o assunto é leve.

**Story time:** Friday of the first week, three days after Episode 02.

### COLD OPEN — UNPACKED
**INT. OLIVIA'S SUBLET — DAWN**

*Estimated duration: 0:45*

A small rented room. Blue light before sunrise.

Two suitcases lie open on the floor, still full. A week in this city, and nothing has been unpacked. A jacket over a chair. A camera bag by the door.

OLIVIA sits on the edge of the bed, already dressed, hair still down.

She reaches under the bed and pulls out a METAL FILM BOX: dented, old, covered in faded LUGGAGE STICKERS from a dozen cities.

She opens it. Rows of glassine sleeves, filed by hand.

Her fingers walk past them without looking, the way you find something you've found many times before.

She draws out ONE STRIP of negatives and holds it up to the window.

We don't see the image. We only see her face, lit by the thin light through the film.

She looks one second too long.

Then she slides the strip back into its place, closes the lid, and rests both hands flat on it.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**TITLE: THE SPACE BETWEEN US**

### SCENE 001 — THE GAP
**INT. THE OLD BRICK FACTORY — MAIN HALL — MORNING**

*Estimated duration: 1:30*

Friday light through the tall windows. Scaffolding inside now, along the east wall.

On an easel: a large print of the BUILDING IN 1928, black and white, every window intact.

CLAIRE stands in front of it, tablet in hand. MAYA beside her, reading over her shoulder.

**CLAIRE**
The commission wants the original frames documented before we replace a single window.

**MAYA**
Then document them.

**CLAIRE**
From what? This is 1928.
*(taps the tablet)*
And this is today. In between, the city archive has nothing.

**MAYA**
Nothing?

**CLAIRE**
Almost a hundred years of nothing.

JUNE passes behind them with a clipboard and two coffees. She doesn't stop.

**JUNE**
Olivia shot every window in this building. Seven years ago.

She keeps walking.

Claire doesn't move.

Across the hall, by the camera cases, OLIVIA has heard it. Her hands pause on a cable.

Claire looks at the tablet. Then at Olivia. Then at the tablet.

She starts typing.

**MAYA**
What are you doing?

**CLAIRE**
Making a request.

**INSERT — CLAIRE'S TABLET:** a new email.
*To: Olivia Hayes. Subject: Archival photographs.*
*"The landmarks commission requires documentation of the original window frames. Please advise. — C. Bennett"*

Send.

Across the hall, Olivia's phone buzzes. She reads. She looks up at Claire, ten feet away.

Claire is looking at her tablet, very busy.

Olivia types.

**INSERT — CLAIRE'S TABLET:** *"Which windows?"*

Claire types.

**INSERT:** *"All of them."*

Olivia types.

**INSERT:** *"Then yes."*

**MAYA**
You're ten feet apart.

**CLAIRE**
It's for the record.

Claire types: *"When?"*

Across the hall, Olivia's thumb hovers over the screen. For a moment she doesn't type anything at all.

June, passing again, sees it. Says nothing.

Olivia types. Sends.

**INSERT:** *"They're in a box. I'll bring it this afternoon."*

Claire reads it twice.

**MAYA**
What kind of box?

Claire doesn't answer.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 002 — THE BOX
**INT. FACTORY — CATERING TABLE — MIDDAY**

*Estimated duration: 1:15*

The folding table with the thermoses. June sets down the METAL FILM BOX next to the sugar.

MAYA, pouring coffee, notices. She tilts her head to read the stickers.

**MAYA**
Lisbon. Detroit. Manila.
*(a beat)*
This box has more airport stickers than I do.

**JUNE**
It's been busy.

Maya reaches for the latch.

June's hand lands flat on the lid first. Not fast. Just there.

**MAYA**
What's in it?

**JUNE**
Seven years.

Maya takes her hand back.

**MAYA**
That's not an answer.

**JUNE**
It's the only one I have.

**MAYA**
You always have more than one answer. I've watched you for a week.

**JUNE**
*(a dry half-smile)*
Then you know I'm not going to give you the other one.

Maya studies her. June holds the look.

**MAYA**
Our deal was to keep them apart during the breaks.

**JUNE**
It was.

**MAYA**
And you're about to put them in a small room with a box called "seven years."

June picks up the box. Tucks it under her arm.

**JUNE**
I'm not putting them anywhere. Your boss asked for it. In writing.

She walks away.

Maya watches her go. Her coffee is overflowing. She doesn't notice.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 003 — LIGHT TABLE
**INT. FACTORY — OLD FOREMAN'S OFFICE — AFTERNOON**

*Estimated duration: 2:30*

A small office off the main hall, with a glass partition looking onto the floor. Dust on the old filing cabinets. The crew uses it as a base.

On the desk: a LIGHT TABLE, glowing white. A loupe. A red grease pencil. A small negative scanner connected to a LAPTOP.

The METAL BOX sits open.

OLIVIA lays out a sleeve of negatives on the glass. CLAIRE stands across from her, the tablet under her arm like a shield.

**OLIVIA**
The east façade. Fourth roll.

She slides the loupe across the table. Claire picks it up and bends over the glass.

Through the loupe: tiny inverted windows, white frames on black brick. Every mullion visible.

**CLAIRE**
*(quietly, already working)*
These are the originals.

**OLIVIA**
Before they boarded up the second floor.

Claire picks up the red grease pencil without asking and marks a small X on the sleeve, over the frames she needs. Neat. Quick. The way she was taught.

Olivia watches the X.

She doesn't say anything.

Claire glances up, catches Olivia looking at the pencil, and understands what Olivia is looking at.

She goes back to the loupe. A touch too fast.

**CLAIRE**
I need the north side too.

**OLIVIA**
Fifth roll.

Another sleeve. Windows, doors, the cast-iron loading canopy.

Then the frames change. The street outside. A storefront across the road, its door open: a hardware store, keys hanging in the window.

**CLAIRE**
That's the candle shop.

**OLIVIA**
It wasn't, then.

Claire moves the loupe along the strip.

Next frame: a kitchen table. A window. A WOMAN laughing, head tilted back, no bangs.

Claire stops.

She knows this picture. Even in negative, even backwards, she knows it.

Olivia feeds the strip into the scanner. On the laptop, the image turns positive: CLAIRE, seven years younger, laughing at something outside the frame.

The same photograph that hangs on Claire's wall.

Long silence.

**CLAIRE**
I have this one.

*(a long beat)*

**OLIVIA**
I know.
*(a beat)*
You have the only print.

Claire looks up at her. A question in her eyes: how do you know that?

She doesn't ask it.

Olivia doesn't offer.

Claire lowers the loupe to the next sleeve, back to the building, to the windows, to work.

Her hand isn't quite steady.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 004 — THREE FRAMES
**INT. FOREMAN'S OFFICE — CONTINUOUS**

*Estimated duration: 1:15*

Claire works through another sleeve. The light table hums.

She stops.

In the middle of a strip of the building, there is a GAP. Three frames cut out, clean, with scissors. The edges are straight. Someone did this carefully.

**CLAIRE**
What was here?

Olivia looks at the gap. She knew it was there.

**OLIVIA**
Those weren't mine to keep.

**CLAIRE**
Whose were they?

*(a long beat)*

**OLIVIA**
Someone who trusted me.

She takes a breath.

**OLIVIA**
*(lower)*
I didn't take care of that.

Claire waits. The silence asks for the rest.

Olivia reaches for the next sleeve and lays it on the glass. Her eyes stay on the windows.

**OLIVIA**
The west side. You'll want these for the cornice.

Claire watches her a moment longer.

Then she bends to the loupe again.

Beyond the glass partition, out of focus, MAYA has stopped walking.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 005 — WHOSE STORY
**INT. FACTORY — MAIN HALL — LATE AFTERNOON**

*Estimated duration: 1:00*

Through the glass partition of the office: Claire and Olivia, bent over the light, not speaking.

MAYA watches from the hall. No smile. JUNE comes to stand beside her, coiling a cable.

**MAYA**
What did she do?

**JUNE**
Not my story.

**MAYA**
Then whose?

June looks through the glass.

**JUNE**
Hers. She's going to tell it.
*(a beat)*
Give her time.

Maya turns to her.

**MAYA**
*(quiet, no joke in it)*
She gave her seven years.

June stops coiling the cable.

She has no answer, and she doesn't pretend to.

Maya looks back at the glass. Inside, Claire glances at her watch.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 006 — THE LAST STRIP
**INT. FOREMAN'S OFFICE — EVENING**

*Estimated duration: 2:00*

The windows of the main hall have gone dark blue. The crew is gone. A single work light on the floor beyond the glass.

The only real light in the office is the LIGHT TABLE, glowing up into two faces.

CLAIRE is still here. Her coat is on the chair. She hasn't put it on.

OLIVIA closes a sleeve and files it in the box.

**OLIVIA**
It's after six.

**CLAIRE**
I know what time it is.

Olivia looks at her. Claire doesn't look up.

There is one sleeve left in the box. Olivia's hand stops on it.

A beat.

She takes it out and lays it on the glass.

Claire bends to the loupe.

Through the loupe, in negative: a bed. A window. Morning light on a sleeping face. Long hair across the pillow. No bangs.

Claire stays bent over the glass for a long time.

Olivia, without being asked, feeds the strip into the scanner.

On the laptop: CLAIRE, asleep, seven years ago. Soft light. Completely unguarded.

Claire straightens slowly. She doesn't look at Olivia.

**CLAIRE**
You took this the morning you left.

*(a long beat)*

**OLIVIA**
Yes.

**CLAIRE**
*(her voice lower)*
You could have woken me.

A long silence. The light table hums.

Olivia looks at the photograph, not at Claire.

**OLIVIA**
If I'd woken you...
*(a breath)*
...I wouldn't have gone.

Claire closes her eyes.

When she opens them, they are wet. ONE TEAR escapes. She turns her face toward the dark glass of the partition before it can fall, and wipes it away with the back of her hand.

Olivia sees it. She doesn't move toward her.

**OLIVIA**
*(almost no voice)*
I've looked at it every year since.

Claire takes a slow breath. She reaches for the strip, to slide it back into its sleeve, to put it away, to end this.

OLIVIA'S HAND COVERS HERS.

Just that. Olivia's hand on Claire's hand, flat on the glowing glass.

One second.

Two.

Claire doesn't pull away.

It is Olivia who lifts her hand first.

Neither of them speaks.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 007 — LEFT BEHIND
**INT. FOREMAN'S OFFICE — NIGHT**

*Estimated duration: 0:45*

Olivia closes the lid of the metal box. Fastens the latch. Puts on her jacket.

At the door, she stops.

**OLIVIA**
Goodnight, Claire.

She goes.

Claire alone. The light table still glowing.

On the glass, where Olivia's hand was: THE LAST STRIP. Not in its sleeve. Not in the box.

Claire looks at the door. Then at the strip.

She picks it up by its edges, the way Olivia taught her, and slides it into the inside pocket of her coat.

She switches off the light table.

Darkness.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 008 — YOU LEFT ONE
**INT. PRODUCTION VAN — NIGHT**

*Estimated duration: 0:45*

JUNE at the wheel, engine off. OLIVIA in the passenger seat, the metal box on her lap.

Rain beginning on the windshield.

June doesn't start the van.

**JUNE**
*(eyes on the road)*
You left one.

*(a beat)*

**OLIVIA**
I know.

June turns her head and looks at her friend.

Olivia is looking at the box. Not sad, exactly. Something closer to having finally put something down.

June understands: it wasn't an accident.

She turns the key. The dashboard lights up.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 009 — DELIVERED
**INT. CLAIRE'S APARTMENT — STUDY — NIGHT**

*Estimated duration: 1:30*

The study. The iMac's cool light. The warm lamp behind. The photo wall in half-darkness.

CLAIRE takes the small old photograph down from the wall, frame and all, and lays it on the desk.

Next to it, she lays the STRIP OF NEGATIVES from her coat.

She holds the strip up against the white glow of the screen. The same morning, both sides of it: the print, and the moment it came from.

She sets it down.

She picks up her phone. Scrolls. Stops.

**INSERT — CLAIRE'S PHONE:** a contact. *Olivia.* An old thread. The last message in it is from seven years ago.

Her thumb hovers.

She types: *"You kept them."*

A long beat.

She sends it.

**CUT TO:**

**INT. PRODUCTION VAN — NIGHT — CONTINUOUS**

The van is parked outside a dark building. Rain on the glass.

On top of the metal box in Olivia's lap, her PHONE lights up.

**INSERT — OLIVIA'S PHONE:** *Claire.* *"You kept them."*

Olivia never deleted the number either.

She looks at the screen for a long time.

June, at the wheel, sees the name and looks away out of the window, giving her privacy.

Olivia types. Stops. Deletes something. Types again.

*"So did you."*

She sends it.

**CUT TO:**

**INT. CLAIRE'S APARTMENT — STUDY — NIGHT — CONTINUOUS**

The phone buzzes on the desk, next to the old photograph and the strip of film.

Claire reads it.

She looks up at the wall of photographs, at the empty space where the small frame used to hang.

She doesn't put it back.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**END OF EPISODE 03**

- **Ambientes novos:** o escritório do capataz (dentro da fábrica, com divisória de vidro para o salão) e o quarto alugado de Olivia (só no cold open).

  - as folhas de negativo em envelopes translúcidos;

  - o scanner com o laptop.

- **Choro:** nível 2 só em Claire, na SC006; Olivia no nível 1.
- **Looks de sexta-feira:** a definir no desenvolvimento visual. Claire precisa de um **casaco com bolso interno** (SC007).
