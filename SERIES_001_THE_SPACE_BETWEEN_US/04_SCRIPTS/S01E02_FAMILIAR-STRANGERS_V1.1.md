### COLD OPEN — REHEARSAL
**INT. CLAIRE'S APARTMENT — BEDROOM — EARLY MORNING**

*Estimated duration: 0:45*

Grey morning light. A full-length mirror.

CLAIRE, dressed and ready, faces her reflection. Phone in hand, stopwatch running.

**CLAIRE**
Restoration is about respecting what came before, while—

She stops the stopwatch. Resets it.

**CLAIRE**
Restoration is about—

Stops it again.

**CLAIRE**
No.

She looks at her blouse. Takes off the blazer. Puts it back on.

Takes it off again and throws it on the bed with real hostility.

**CLAIRE**
*(to the mirror, precise)*
Restoration is about deciding what deserves to stay.

She checks the stopwatch. Four seconds.

A satisfied nod. Then she catches her own eye in the mirror, and the satisfaction falters.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**TITLE: THE SPACE BETWEEN US**

### SCENE 001 — THE BUILDING
**EXT./INT. THE OLD BRICK FACTORY — MORNING**

*Estimated duration: 1:30*

A century-old brick factory on a quiet Manhattan street. Scaffolding wraps one side. Tall arched windows, half of them boarded.

Inside: dust in the light, exposed brick, iron columns, the raw shell of a building waiting to become something else.

A documentary crew is already setting up: light stands, cases, cables. JUNE PARK directs traffic with a clipboard.

CLAIRE enters in a white hard hat, MAYA beside her in another.

**MAYA**
You look like you're about to inspect a mine.

**CLAIRE**
I look like someone who read the safety report.

OLIVIA HAYES crosses the floor toward them, camera bag on her shoulder. No hard hat.

Claire holds one out without a word.

Olivia takes it. Puts it on. Doesn't argue.

**OLIVIA**
Good morning.

**CLAIRE**
I prepared a protocol.

She hands Olivia a single typed page.

**OLIVIA**
*(reading)*
"Questions submitted in advance. Hard hats in all active areas. The interviewee reserves the right to review her own footage."

She looks up.

**OLIVIA**
That's very reasonable.

**CLAIRE**
I'm a very reasonable person.

**MAYA**
*(aside, to June)*
She is not.

Olivia hands Claire her own sheet: the interview questions.

Claire scans it. Stops.

**CLAIRE**
Question seven is blank.

**OLIVIA**
That one's spontaneous.

**CLAIRE**
Spontaneity wasn't in the protocol.

**OLIVIA**
Neither was the hard hat. I'm wearing it.

Claire opens her mouth. Closes it.

Behind them, a work light flickers and dies. A crew member looks around, lost.

Without looking, Olivia walks to a rusted panel half-hidden behind a column, opens it, and flips a breaker. The light comes back.

**JUNE**
*(to the crew)*
We spent all of yesterday looking for that.

Olivia crosses back to the set.

Claire watches her go. Then looks at the panel. Then back at Olivia.

She says nothing.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 002 — LIGHTS
**INT. FACTORY — INTERVIEW SET — CONTINUOUS**

*Estimated duration: 1:30*

A simple set by the tall windows: a chair, a soft key light, a camera on a tripod.

Claire sits. Olivia adjusts the light, moving it from Claire's right to her left.

**CLAIRE**
What are you doing?

**OLIVIA**
Moving the light.

**CLAIRE**
I can see that.

**OLIVIA**
You have a preferred side.

**CLAIRE**
I don't have a good side.

**OLIVIA**
I said preferred.

Olivia sets a glass of water on a small crate within Claire's reach.

Claire looks at the glass. She hadn't asked.

She drinks anyway.

Olivia steps behind the camera and checks the frame. Claire glances at the monitor.

**CLAIRE**
The column is cutting through my head.

**OLIVIA**
I was just about to fix that.

She shifts the tripod a few inches. On the monitor, the column slides out of frame.

**CLAIRE**
Of course you were.

Olivia picks up a small lavalier microphone and comes around the camera.

She stops an arm's length away.

**OLIVIA**
May I?

A beat.

**CLAIRE**
Yes.

Olivia clips the microphone to Claire's collar and tucks the cable inside her blouse, careful, quick, professional.

For one second, they are very close.

Neither moves.

Then Olivia steps back.

**OLIVIA**
Say something.

**CLAIRE**
*(flat)*
Something.

Olivia checks the headphones.

**OLIVIA**
Perfect.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 003 — ON THE RECORD
**INT. FACTORY — INTERVIEW SET — CONTINUOUS**

*Estimated duration: 2:15*

The red recording light on the camera glows.

Behind the monitor, Maya and June stand side by side, arms folded identically.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
What is restoration about?

**CLAIRE**
Restoration is about deciding what deserves to stay.

Four seconds. Word-perfect. The rehearsal paid off.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
Tell me about this building.

**CLAIRE**
The structure dates from 1928. Load-bearing brick, cast-iron columns, original timber floors on the upper levels. Our approach is to preserve the envelope and adapt the interior for community use.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
What do you keep, and what do you let go?

**CLAIRE**
We keep what's structurally sound and historically significant. We let go of what can't be saved.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
How do you know what can't be saved?

Claire hesitates. Half a second.

**CLAIRE**
You test it.

Behind the monitor, Maya slowly turns her head toward June. June does not turn hers.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
What happens to the things people leave behind?

**CLAIRE**
*(carefully)*
Sometimes they're just abandoned. Sometimes someone keeps them.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
Which is it here?

**CLAIRE**
Here... some structures look intact from the outside. You don't know what's damaged until you open the walls.

Silence.

Claire hears herself.

**CLAIRE**
I'd like to do that again.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
Which part?

**CLAIRE**
All of it.

**MAYA**
*(whispering to June)*
Is this a documentary or a deposition?

**JUNE**
*(whispering)*
Yes.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
Last question.

Claire straightens. Ready.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
Why did you stay in New York?

Claire looks past the camera, straight at Olivia.

**CLAIRE**
Next question.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
That was the last one.

A beat.

**CLAIRE**
Then we're done.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 004 — DID YOU KNOW?
**INT. FACTORY — INTERVIEW SET — CONTINUOUS**

*Estimated duration: 1:45*

The red light goes out.

**JUNE**
Fifteen minutes, everyone.

The crew drifts away. June gives Maya a look; Maya gives one back. They leave together.

Claire unclips the microphone herself. Olivia doesn't help.

Claire holds out the little microphone. Olivia takes it.

**CLAIRE**
Did you know?

Olivia winds the cable around her fingers.

**CLAIRE**
When you knocked on my door. Did you know it was my project?

Olivia stops winding.

**OLIVIA**
Yes.

Claire absorbs it. Nods once, very slightly, as if confirming a measurement.

**CLAIRE**
And you still came to my door first.

**OLIVIA**
I didn't want the first time I saw you to be across a conference table.

**CLAIRE**
So you decided where it would be.

**OLIVIA**
I thought it would be easier.

**CLAIRE**
For whom?

Olivia doesn't answer. That is the answer.

Claire stands. Smooths her blouse. Her voice stays level; it costs her something.

**CLAIRE**
You don't get to decide that for me.

A beat.

**CLAIRE**
Not again.

She walks away across the factory floor, past the columns, into the light from the tall windows.

Olivia stays where she is, the microphone cable still wound around her fingers.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 005 — THE HANDLERS
**INT. FACTORY — CATERING TABLE — CONTINUOUS**

*Estimated duration: 1:15*

A folding table with coffee urns and a box of pastries nobody trusts.

Maya pours coffee. June arrives and takes a cup without asking.

**MAYA**
Is she always like this?

**JUNE**
Which one?

**MAYA**
Yours.

**JUNE**
Only since Thursday.

**MAYA**
Mine's been like this since I met her.

June considers this.

**JUNE**
That's a long time to be like this.

**MAYA**
You have no idea.

They drink.

**MAYA**
Proposal. During breaks, we keep them apart.

**JUNE**
Separate tables.

**MAYA**
Separate floors, if possible.

**JUNE**
Agreed.

They shake on it.

Over June's shoulder, Maya spots something. June turns.

Across the floor, Claire and Olivia have both ended up at the same tall window, at opposite ends, very carefully not looking at each other.

**MAYA**
That lasted eleven seconds.

**JUNE**
Twelve.

Maya studies June for a moment.

**MAYA**
You know something.

**JUNE**
I know a lot of things.

**MAYA**
About her.

June sips her coffee.

**JUNE**
The residents' interviews start at two. Your architect should be there.

**MAYA**
That's not an answer.

**JUNE**
It's a schedule.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 006 — THE MAN WHO REMEMBERED
**INT. FACTORY — INTERVIEW SET — AFTERNOON**

*Estimated duration: 1:30*

The afternoon light has moved. A new subject sits in the chair.

ABEL ORTIZ, 74. Cardigan, pressed shirt, the hands of a man who fixed things for a living.

Claire sits to one side with a notebook, representing the project. Olivia is behind the camera.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
How long did you have the store, Mr. Ortiz?

**ABEL**
Forty-one years. Hardware. Right across the street.

He points through the window. A glass storefront that now sells candles.

**ABEL**
Every key in this neighborhood, I cut. Every lock, I fixed.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
And this building?

**ABEL**
I watched it empty out, floor by floor.

He squints past the lens, toward Olivia.

He frowns. Leans slightly to one side to see around the camera.

**ABEL**
You.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
I'm sorry?

**ABEL**
You're the girl with the camera.

Claire looks up from her notebook.

**ABEL**
You came back.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
I think you might be confusing me with—

**ABEL**
You had longer hair. You asked about the letters.

A very small silence.

Claire looks at Olivia. Olivia doesn't look at Claire.

**OLIVIA (O.S.)**
*(evenly)*
What did you do when the building closed, Mr. Ortiz?

Abel studies her a moment longer. Then he lets it go, the way old men let go of things they intend to remember later.

**ABEL**
I kept the store open three more years. Out of spite.

Claire writes nothing in her notebook. She is watching Olivia.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 007 — SIX O'CLOCK
**INT. FACTORY — STAIRWELL — GOLDEN HOUR**

*Estimated duration: 1:30*

An iron stairwell. Low gold light through a dusty window.

Olivia carries a light stand down the stairs. Claire's footsteps catch up behind her.

**CLAIRE**
He knew you.

Olivia stops on the landing.

**OLIVIA**
I photographed this street once. A long time ago.

**CLAIRE**
When?

**OLIVIA**
Around the time I left.

Claire takes that in.

The question is right there: the real one, the one she has waited seven years to ask.

She looks at her watch.

**CLAIRE**
It's five fifty-eight.

**OLIVIA**
Is that a problem?

**CLAIRE**
No personal conversations during work hours.

**OLIVIA**
Your rule.

**CLAIRE**
My rule.

They stand there.

Somewhere below, a door bangs. The light shifts on the brick.

Claire watches the second hand.

Olivia watches Claire.

The watch reads 6:00.

Claire looks up. Olivia waits, ready for whatever comes.

Claire opens her mouth.

**CLAIRE**
Goodnight, Olivia.

She walks past her down the stairs.

Olivia stays on the landing, the light stand in her hand, as Claire's footsteps fade.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 008 — HALF THE TRUTH
**INT. PRODUCTION VAN — NIGHT**

*Estimated duration: 0:45*

Equipment cases, a laptop glowing, the street outside the windshield.

June closes a case. Olivia sits in the passenger seat, staring at nothing.

**JUNE**
Did you tell her?

**OLIVIA**
She asked if I knew it was her project.

**JUNE**
And?

**OLIVIA**
I told her the truth.

June waits.

**JUNE**
About that.

Olivia turns to the window.

**OLIVIA**
About that.

June starts the engine.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 009 — RAW FOOTAGE
**INT. CLAIRE'S APARTMENT — STUDY — NIGHT**

*Estimated duration: 1:30*

The study: desk, iMac, shelves, a wall of framed photographs.

Claire scrolls through a folder of video files. A shared link: S01_INTERVIEW_BENNETT_RAW.

She plays her own interview. Watches herself say:

**CLAIRE (ON SCREEN)**
You don't know what's damaged until you open the walls.

She winces. Skips ahead.

The last file in the folder has a different name: B-ROLL_BREAK_03.

She clicks it.

On screen: Claire, earlier that day, during the break, at the tall window. Maya says something off-camera. Claire laughs, fully, unguarded, head tilted back.

She didn't know she was being filmed.

Claire stops the video. The frozen image stays on the screen.

Slowly, she turns her chair.

On the wall of photographs, among buildings and city streets: one small photo, older than the others. Claire, seven years younger, no bangs, laughing at something outside the frame.

The same angle. The same laugh.

Claire looks from the wall to the screen and back.

She moves the cursor to the file. Right-click. DELETE.

Her finger rests on the mouse.

She doesn't click.

She switches off the screen.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**END OF EPISODE 02**
