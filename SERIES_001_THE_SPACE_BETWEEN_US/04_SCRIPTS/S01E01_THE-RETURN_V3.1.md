### SCENE 001 — EVERYTHING UNDER CONTROL
**INT. ARCHITECTURE STUDIO — LATE AFTERNOON**

*Estimated duration: 1:35*

A sleek Manhattan architecture studio. Glass walls, architectural models, carefully arranged samples. The city glows in the late-afternoon light.

CLAIRE BENNETT, 35, elegant and composed, stands over a large architectural model.

Beside her is MAYA REED, 34, a senior architect who manages the firm's projects, and Claire's closest friend at the firm. Sharp, warm, and entirely unimpressed by Claire's attempts at intimidation.

Claire adjusts a tiny model tree.

**MAYA**
You know that's a tree, right?

**CLAIRE**
I'm aware.

**MAYA**
A tiny, plastic tree.

**CLAIRE**
It's blocking the entrance.

**MAYA**
It's four millimeters tall.

**CLAIRE**
And somehow still in the wrong place.

Maya watches Claire move the tree approximately half an inch.

**MAYA**
There. Civilization is saved.

Claire steps back, studying the model.

**CLAIRE**
The entrance reads better now.

**MAYA**
The entrance could win an award. You, on the other hand, need a weekend.

**CLAIRE**
I have a weekend.

**MAYA**
You scheduled laundry for Saturday at nine and grocery shopping at eleven.

**CLAIRE**
That's called having a life.

**MAYA**
That's called having a spreadsheet.

Claire ignores her and closes the model's protective case.

Maya places a folder on the desk.

**MAYA**
Tomorrow's presentation is ready. The redevelopment board, the community liaison, the documentary team—

**CLAIRE**
The documentary team?

**MAYA**
Communications hired someone to document the restoration. Apparently, buildings need origin stories now.

**CLAIRE**
Buildings have histories.

**MAYA**
And apparently, histories need cameras.

Claire opens the folder.

**CLAIRE**
As long as nobody films me pretending to enjoy a networking event.

**MAYA**
That's my entire marketing strategy.

Claire gives her a look.

**MAYA**
Go home, Claire.

**CLAIRE**
I am going home.

**MAYA**
Without taking the building with you.

Claire reluctantly puts down the folder.

**CLAIRE**
Fine.

She picks up her phone instead.

**MAYA**
That's worse.

Claire walks toward the exit.

**CLAIRE**
Good night, Maya.

**MAYA**
Try doing something spontaneous!

**CLAIRE**
I'll consider scheduling it.

Maya smiles as Claire disappears into the elevator.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 002 — A PERFECTLY ORDINARY NIGHT
**INT. CLAIRE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

*Estimated duration: 1:25*

Rain traces the windows of Claire's beautiful Manhattan apartment.

Warm amber lighting reflects off dark wood, bronze accents, and polished marble. A cooler blue glow from a light near the window gives the room its distinctive cinematic contrast.

Claire enters, sets her keys in a small ceramic dish, and hangs her coat.

She notices one of the keys is slightly crooked.

She straightens it.

Her phone BUZZES.

**PHONE SCREEN — DELIVERY UPDATE**

*Your order is on its way.*

Claire moves into the kitchen and opens the refrigerator.

Inside: sparkling water, neatly arranged vegetables, expensive cheese, and almost nothing resembling dinner.

She studies the contents.

**CLAIRE**
*(to herself)*
Excellent. A refrigerator full of ambition.

She takes out a container of leftover pasta, checks the date, and puts it back.

Her phone RINGS.

MAYA appears on the screen.

Claire answers on speaker.

**CLAIRE**
What?

**MAYA (V.O.)**
That's a lovely way to greet someone.

**CLAIRE**
I assumed it was an emergency.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
It is. I'm checking whether you've eaten something that wasn't prepared by a restaurant.

**CLAIRE**
I had a salad.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
When?

**CLAIRE**
Recently.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
Claire.

**CLAIRE**
Wednesday.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
It's Thursday.

**CLAIRE**
Time is a social construct.

Maya laughs.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
Good night, you terrifying woman.

**CLAIRE**
Good night.

The call ends.

Claire places her phone on the counter.

A delivery notification appears.

*Your courier is nearby.*

Claire exhales, relieved.

**CLAIRE**
Finally.

She reaches for a glass of water.

A quiet KNOCK.

Claire glances toward the entrance.

Another notification lights up her phone.

She walks toward the door.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 003 — THE RETURN 🔒 LOCKED
**INT. CLAIRE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

*Estimated duration: 2:30* (o teste de 68 s é referência de decupagem, não a versão final)

Rain taps softly against the windows.

Claire walks toward the front door, glancing at her phone. Another delivery notification.

She opens the door.

Olivia stands in the hallway, her coat damp from the rain.

Claire freezes.

**CLAIRE**
Olivia?

**OLIVIA**
Hi, Claire.

A long silence. Claire's fingers tighten around the edge of the door.

**CLAIRE**
Seven years.

**OLIVIA**
I know.

**CLAIRE**
And you thought you could just show up?

Olivia looks down briefly.

**OLIVIA**
No. I thought you'd close the door.

Claire almost smiles. Almost.

**CLAIRE**
I still might.

Olivia nods, accepting it.

**OLIVIA**
You cut your hair.

Claire looks at her, incredulous.

**CLAIRE**
That's what you want to talk about?

**OLIVIA**
No.

A beat.

**OLIVIA**
It's just the first thing I noticed.

Claire's expression changes. For a moment, the anger gives way to something familiar.

**CLAIRE**
You don't get to do that.

**OLIVIA**
Do what?

**CLAIRE**
Talk to me like you still know me.

Olivia meets her eyes.

**OLIVIA**
I know.

Claire waits.

**OLIVIA**
But I remember you.

The rain grows louder. Claire looks away, struggling to maintain her composure.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

> Regras travadas: Olivia não entra no apartamento · Claire não a convida · nenhuma fala nova · sem humor artificial.

### SCENE 004 — THE MORNING AFTER
**INT. CLAIRE'S APARTMENT — MORNING**

*Estimated duration: 1:15*

A soft morning light fills the apartment.

Claire stands in the kitchen, staring at a coffee machine.

She presses a button.

Nothing happens.

She presses it again.

Still nothing.

**CLAIRE**
Oh, come on.

She looks down.

The machine isn't plugged in.

She plugs it in and immediately looks around, as if someone might have witnessed the incident.

While the machine warms up, Claire picks up her phone.

She scrolls to a contact. OLIVIA. An old number she never deleted.

Her thumb hovers over it.

She locks the screen and sets the phone face down on the counter.

Her phone RINGS.

MAYA.

**CLAIRE**
Hello.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
You sound strange.

**CLAIRE**
Good morning to you too.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
Did you sleep?

**CLAIRE**
Of course.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
How many hours?

**CLAIRE**
That's a very invasive question.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
So, none.

Claire pours coffee into a cup.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
Are you okay?

Claire hesitates.

**CLAIRE**
Someone came by last night.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
Someone?

**CLAIRE**
Olivia.

Silence.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
Olivia Olivia?

**CLAIRE**
How many Olivias do you think I know?

**MAYA (V.O.)**
I was hoping at least two.

Claire closes her eyes.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
What did she want?

**CLAIRE**
I didn't ask.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
Claire.

**CLAIRE**
I was busy being shocked.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
Fair.

Claire takes a sip of coffee and grimaces.

She has forgotten to put the cup under the machine correctly. Most of the coffee is on the counter.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
You're going to be late.

**CLAIRE**
I'm never late.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
You're already seven minutes late.

Claire checks the clock.

**CLAIRE**
I hate you.

**MAYA (V.O.)**
Love you too.

**CUT TO:**

> Nota de continuidade: Claire "pours coffee into a cup" e depois descobre que a xícara estava mal posicionada. A ação precisa ser encenada de modo que as duas coisas sejam compatíveis (ex.: ela serve sem olhar, distraída).

### SCENE 005 — THE WOMAN WHO CAME BACK
**INT. NEW YORK CAFÉ — MORNING**

*Estimated duration: 1:35*

A small café in Manhattan. Warm sunlight filters through the windows.

OLIVIA HAYES, 33, sits at a corner table with a camera bag beside her.

Across from her is JUNE PARK, 34, a documentary producer with an observant expression and a talent for asking inconvenient questions.

June reviews a production schedule on her laptop.

**JUNE**
We have access to the restoration site starting Monday. Interviews begin Tuesday. And the architectural team wants a meeting today.

Olivia nods, distracted.

**JUNE**
Olivia?

**OLIVIA**
I'm listening.

**JUNE**
Then what did I just say?

**OLIVIA**
Something about architecture.

**JUNE**
Remarkable. You heard an entire noun.

Olivia smiles faintly.

June closes the laptop halfway.

**JUNE**
You went to see her.

Olivia looks up.

**OLIVIA**
Who?

**JUNE**
The woman you've been pretending not to think about since we landed.

**OLIVIA**
That's a very long description.

**JUNE**
Is it inaccurate?

Olivia stirs her coffee.

**OLIVIA**
I went to her apartment.

**JUNE**
And?

**OLIVIA**
She opened the door.

**JUNE**
Promising.

**OLIVIA**
She looked like she wanted to murder me.

**JUNE**
Less promising.

**OLIVIA**
She didn't close it immediately.

**JUNE**
A romantic triumph.

Olivia laughs despite herself.

**JUNE**
Did you tell her why you left?

**OLIVIA**
No.

**JUNE**
Did you apologize?

Olivia pauses.

**OLIVIA**
I didn't get that far.

June studies her.

**JUNE**
You can't spend seven years rehearsing a conversation and then expect the other person to follow your script.

Olivia looks down.

**OLIVIA**
I know.

**JUNE**
And the project?

**OLIVIA**
What about it?

**JUNE**
You know there are old records we still need to review.

Olivia's expression shifts.

**OLIVIA**
I know that too.

June opens her laptop again.

**JUNE**
One disaster at a time, then.

Olivia finally smiles.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 006 — PROFESSIONAL BOUNDARIES
**INT. ARCHITECTURE STUDIO — DAY**

*Estimated duration: 1:45*

A conference room with glass walls and a large architectural model at its center.

Claire stands beside a presentation screen. Maya and two members of the redevelopment team are seated nearby.

Claire is midway through a presentation.

**CLAIRE**
The goal isn't simply to preserve the building. It's to make the restoration useful to the neighborhood without erasing what was there before.

She changes slides.

An image of the old building appears.

**CLAIRE**
The original structure has been part of this community for almost a century. Its history matters.

Maya nods.

**MAYA**
Which is why communications commissioned an independent documentary.

Claire stiffens almost imperceptibly.

**MAYA**
They'll be recording the restoration, interviewing residents, and documenting the design process.

**CLAIRE**
Wonderful.

Her tone suggests otherwise.

**MAYA**
The production team is here this afternoon.

Maya changes the slide.

**DOCUMENTARY PRODUCTION — OLIVIA HAYES / JUNE PARK**

Claire stops breathing for a fraction of a second.

**MAYA**
Claire?

**CLAIRE**
Yes.

**MAYA**
You were saying?

Claire looks at Olivia's name on the screen.

**CLAIRE**
I was saying the structural integrity of the eastern façade is our primary concern.

**MAYA**
We're on the documentary slide.

**CLAIRE**
Right.

A beat.

**CLAIRE**
Then the documentary is our primary concern.

Maya narrows her eyes.

**MAYA**
Are you feeling all right?

**CLAIRE**
Perfectly.

She clicks the remote.

The slide changes to a detailed floor plan.

**CLAIRE**
Now. If we could return to something that makes sense.

Maya watches her.

**MAYA**
Something tells me this is going to be interesting.

Claire doesn't answer.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 007 — STRICTLY PROFESSIONAL
**INT. ARCHITECTURE STUDIO — DAY**

*Estimated duration: 1:25*

The conference room is emptying.

Claire stands by the window, arms crossed, staring out at Manhattan.

Maya approaches.

**MAYA**
You know her.

**CLAIRE**
I know a lot of people.

**MAYA**
You nearly declared war on a PowerPoint slide.

**CLAIRE**
I dislike unnecessary surprises.

**MAYA**
Claire.

Claire turns.

**CLAIRE**
She's my ex.

Maya waits.

**CLAIRE**
The ex.

**MAYA**
Oh.

A beat.

**MAYA**
Oh, that's unfortunate.

**CLAIRE**
Thank you for your support.

The glass door opens.

Olivia enters carrying a camera bag.

She stops when she sees Claire.

**OLIVIA**
Hi.

**CLAIRE**
Hello.

Maya looks between them.

**MAYA**
Well. I'll let you two discuss the production schedule.

She exits a little too quickly.

Claire turns to Olivia.

**CLAIRE**
I want to make something very clear.

**OLIVIA**
Okay.

**CLAIRE**
This is a professional environment.

**OLIVIA**
I noticed the desks.

**CLAIRE**
Olivia.

**OLIVIA**
Sorry.

**CLAIRE**
There will be boundaries.

**OLIVIA**
Of course.

**CLAIRE**
No personal conversations during work hours.

**OLIVIA**
Understood.

**CLAIRE**
No surprises.

Olivia considers this.

**OLIVIA**
I can try.

**CLAIRE**
That wasn't reassuring.

**OLIVIA**
I was going for honest.

A brief silence.

Claire looks away before Olivia can see the beginning of a smile.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 008 — THE FIRST RULE
**INT. ARCHITECTURE STUDIO — LATER**

*Estimated duration: 1:45*

Olivia photographs an architectural model from different angles.

Claire works at a nearby table, pretending not to notice.

The camera shutter clicks.

Again.

And again.

**CLAIRE**
Do you have to do that?

**OLIVIA**
Take photographs?

**CLAIRE**
So many.

**OLIVIA**
I'm a photographer.

**CLAIRE**
I'm aware of your profession.

Olivia lowers the camera.

**OLIVIA**
You always hated that sound.

**CLAIRE**
I don't hate the sound.

A shutter clicks.

Claire looks up.

**CLAIRE**
I hate unnecessary repetition.

**OLIVIA**
Noted.

Olivia changes position and photographs the model from a lower angle.

Claire watches her for a moment.

**CLAIRE**
You're photographing the wrong side.

**OLIVIA**
Am I?

**CLAIRE**
The light is better from the west.

Olivia looks toward the windows.

**OLIVIA**
You're right.

She moves to the other side.

A beat.

**OLIVIA**
You still do that.

**CLAIRE**
Do what?

**OLIVIA**
Pretend you're not helping.

Claire returns to her laptop.

**CLAIRE**
I'm protecting the quality of the project.

**OLIVIA**
Naturally.

Olivia photographs the model.

**CLAIRE**
And for the record, I don't always do that.

**OLIVIA**
Of course not.

**CLAIRE**
You're being sarcastic.

**OLIVIA**
I learned from the best.

Claire almost laughs.

Almost.

Olivia notices.

Neither comments.

For a brief moment, the room feels familiar.

Then Claire closes her laptop.

**CLAIRE**
We should review the schedule.

**OLIVIA**
Strictly professionally?

**CLAIRE**
Obviously.

**OLIVIA**
Wouldn't dream of anything else.

Claire looks at her.

Olivia smiles.

Claire looks away, but this time she can't quite hide her own smile.

**CUT TO:**

### SCENE 009 — THIS CHANGES NOTHING
**INT. ARCHITECTURE STUDIO — EVENING**

*Estimated duration: 1:15*

The office is nearly empty.

Claire sits at her desk, reviewing the documentary production schedule.

Maya approaches with two takeaway coffees.

**MAYA**
Peace offering.

**CLAIRE**
For what?

**MAYA**
For whatever I did by not knowing your ex was the documentary photographer.

Claire accepts the coffee.

**CLAIRE**
You didn't do anything.

**MAYA**
Good. Because I was planning to defend myself vigorously.

Claire scans the schedule.

**CLAIRE**
Three weeks?

**MAYA**
Minimum.

**CLAIRE**
Of filming?

**MAYA**
Of working together.

Claire puts down the document.

**CLAIRE**
This changes nothing.

**MAYA**
Of course.

**CLAIRE**
We're adults.

**MAYA**
Absolutely.

**CLAIRE**
We can be professional.

**MAYA**
Without question.

Claire looks up.

**CLAIRE**
You're enjoying this.

**MAYA**
A little.

Claire sighs.

Across the studio, Olivia is packing her equipment.

June approaches with a slim archival folder.

**JUNE**
I found the original acquisition records.

Olivia takes the folder.

Inside are old photographs, property documents, and a familiar company name.

Her expression changes.

**JUNE**
Is that the one?

Olivia studies a document dated seven years earlier.

**OLIVIA**
Yeah.

June waits.

**JUNE**
Does Claire know?

Olivia looks across the room.

Claire is laughing at something Maya has said.

For a moment, Olivia simply watches her.

**OLIVIA**
Not yet.

Olivia closes the folder.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

## Análise de showrunner (registrada na V3.0)
- **SC001, o humor de Claire:** a implicância com a árvore da maquete estabelece seu perfeccionismo de forma engraçada, sem torná-la antipática.
- **SC003, o coração do piloto:** o silêncio, o olhar de reconhecimento e "But I remember you" são o primeiro grande impacto emocional da série.
- **SC008, a química que conquista:** o público começa a sorrir com as duas. Claire tenta manter distância; Olivia ainda sabe como fazê-la perder a compostura.

## Progressão emocional do piloto (escala 0–10)
Abre com humor de personalidade (SC001–SC002), mergulha no drama no reencontro (SC003), volta à comédia com consequências (SC004–SC005), estabelece a dinâmica de comédia romântica (SC006–SC008) e fecha com gancho de mistério (SC009).
