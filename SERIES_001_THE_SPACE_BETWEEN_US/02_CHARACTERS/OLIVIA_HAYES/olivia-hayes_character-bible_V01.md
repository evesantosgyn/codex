### 4.2 Blocos de direção (inglês)
- **Observation:** *Olivia watches the world like a photographer: curious, warm, framing what moves her, hands near her camera.*
- **Love:** *Olivia still loves Claire and doesn't hide her eyes: she looks a second too long and only looks away when it hurts. A breath before Claire's name. She says the true things while looking somewhere else.*
- **Guilt:** *Olivia's eyes stay on the table, never on Claire. Her breath is held, then let out long. She busies her hands with work and changes the subject, voice lower, sentences unfinished.*
- **Sadness:** *Her eyes fill and she does not hide it; her gaze goes to the window; her fingers stop on whatever they hold. Voice husky and slow.*
- **Fear:** *Her eyes flick to the door or her bag; a short nervous laugh; her hand tightens on the strap; ready to leave.*
- **Humor:** *Affectionate irony: a spark in the eyes, a small provoking gesture; the smile breaks halfway, never a big open laugh with Claire.*
- **Vulnerable:** *She faces Claire with full eyes and empty open hands, voice almost a whisper, nothing to defend.*
- **Never neutral:** *Olivia never says a personal line with a neutral face: her eyes fill or soften before her mouth opens; her mouth barely opens.*
