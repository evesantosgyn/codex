## 4. Mapa de interpretação

> **Regra-mãe [C] (V70):** o controle de Claire é **a armadura de quem ainda sente**. Em toda emoção, **a ordem é sempre a mesma: os olhos traem primeiro, depois a respiração, a boca por último.** A voz quase nunca sobe. Quanto maior a emoção, **menos ela se mexe**.

### 4.1 Tabela por emoção

### 4.2 Blocos de direção (inglês, neutros, para colar no card ou no prompt)
- **Control:** *Claire is in control and wants everyone to see it. Upright, still, precise hands, dry voice, short declarative sentences. Her eyes are the only thing that might give her away.*
- **Love (hidden):** *Claire still loves Olivia and will not admit it. She looks at Olivia only when Olivia is not looking, and looks away when caught. Her voice drops slightly and catches on Olivia's name, with a small breath before it. She covers tenderness with a technical correction.*
- **Sadness:** *Claire's sadness is still. She stops moving, her eyes fixed on an object, not a person. A long exhale through the nose. Slower, lower voice, never tearful. Eyes glistening (level 1), no tear.*
- **Cold anger:** *Claire does not raise her voice. She goes quieter, slower, every word separated. Her eyes hold, blinking slowly. She stops moving.*
- **Fear:** *Claire's eyes flick to the exit or her watch. Shallow breath. She folds her arms and steers back to work, speaking a little faster.*
- **Almost smile:** *The corner of Claire's mouth starts to lift and she catches it before it becomes a smile; she looks down to hide it.*
- **Vulnerable:** *For once Claire does not look away. She holds Olivia's eyes for two or three seconds, her hands still and open. Her voice is almost a whisper; the sentence stays unfinished.*
- **Comedy:** *Claire is completely serious in the face; the comedy is in her timing: a beat before the line, ironic emphasis, legal formality about small things.*

### 4.4 Comportamentos proibidos (rejeitar o take)
- Rir aberto, gargalhar, sorriso largo sem motivo.
- Gritar, gesticular muito, chorar soluçando (fora de um pico autorizado).
- Olhar fixo para Olivia por muito tempo sem desviar (exceto em vulnerabilidade autorizada).
- Postura largada (exceto em casa, sozinha).
- Expressão neutra numa fala pessoal com Olivia (V70: nada é neutro).
- Falar rápido numa cena de amor ou tristeza (E5: ~2 palavras/s).
