**Blocos de direção (inglês):**
- **Deadpan:** *June's comedy is entirely in her timing: a perfectly still face, irony only in her eyes and one corner of her mouth; short dry lines.*

- **Silent truth:** *When the truth lands, June's hands stop. She has no answer and doesn't pretend to; her eyes lower; her mouth stays closed.*
